# ANSI Shadow notice

`ansi-shadow.flf` is the figlet font **"ANSI Shadow"**.

- Its header names no author (`Font Author: ?`) and points to the TheDraw (TDF)
  font collection at roysac.com
  (https://web.archive.org/web/20120819044459/http://www.roysac.com/thedrawfonts-tdf.asp),
  from which it was converted with patorjk.com's FIGfont editor.
- It is widely redistributed with figlet.js, pyfiglet and similar packages.
- No explicit licence was found. It is included here as-is, unmodified.

To use a different figlet font, point `tagline.font` in `banner.config.mjs` at
any `.flf` file; this one is then not used at all.
